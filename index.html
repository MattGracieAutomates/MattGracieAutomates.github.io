Deal memos

Deal memos is a private tool used by one person to analyze rental property listings in Sydney, Nova Scotia. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Once a day, the tool writes a one-page due-diligence memo for each property worth a closer look, as a Google Doc in its owner's own Google Drive. The memo summarizes why the property ranks, its red flags, what is still unverified, and a draft email the owner may choose to send to the listing agent.

The tool asks Google for one permission only: to see, create and edit the Google Drive files it creates itself (the drive.file scope). It cannot see any other file in the Drive.
